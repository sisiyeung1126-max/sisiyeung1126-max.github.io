繁星字卡 / Star Cards

Privacy Policy · Last updated: 22 September 2026

English

Star Cards (繁星字卡) is a parent-and-child flashcard app for ages about 3–6. Chinese families can learn English; anyone can learn Chinese (Traditional / Simplified, Cantonese / Mandarin / English speech).

  • We do not create child accounts.
  • We do not collect personal data on a server.
  • Progress and settings stay on this device (UserDefaults only).
  • There are no ads, no third-party ad or analytics SDKs, and no tracking.
  • Speech uses the device’s built-in AVSpeechSynthesizer. Audio does not leave the device for our servers — we have none.
  • Purchases: this build’s paywall is a device demo. It does not charge the App Store. A future paid version will use Apple In-App Purchase.
  • External links and the paywall are behind a parent gate (simple addition).
  • Deleting the app, or clearing local progress in parent settings, clears on-device data.

Privacy contact: sisiyeung1126@icloud.com
